Questions

Is this subletting?

Yes, with your written permission. BA Properties signs the lease as your tenant and hosts furnished stays in the property. Nothing happens without your approval in the lease.

What if it doesn’t book?

You still get paid. The rent is ours to pay on the agreed date, whether or not the property is booked.

Who pays for damage?

Our guests are screened and monitored, our team cleans after every stay, and the property is covered by our liability insurance while we lease it. We take care of wear and tear.

How much rent will you pay?

It depends on the property, its location and its condition. We’ll look at it and give you a number in writing. Step one is the form above.

Which areas do you work in?

St. Louis city and county, and Indianapolis. If you’re elsewhere, send it anyway and we’ll tell you honestly if it fits.
